Step-by-Step Breakdown of the Process

Initial Consultation and Goal Setting

Before starting the three-day marathon, we have an important preparatory phase. At the first meeting:

Familiarizing ourselves with the client's business:

  • We study the specifics of the industry
  • Analyze the target audience
  • Identify competitive advantages

Define project objectives:

  • Prioritize
  • Formulate measurable results
  • Discuss technical limitations

Agree on the format of work:

  • Determine communication channels
  • Set time for feedback
  • Assign responsible persons

Day 1: Research and Ideation

The first day is entirely dedicated to research and idea generation. Our team:

Conducts market analysis:

  • Studies trends in the industry
  • Examines competitors' solutions
  • Collects examples of successful cases

Organizes brainstorming sessions:

  • Generates different concepts
  • Creates quick sketches
  • Generates mood boards

Prepares first visual proposals:

  • Develops basic structure
  • Defines basic design elements
  • Creates rough layouts

Day 2: Prototyping and Feedback

The second day is the time for detailed development of the chosen concept:

Prototyping:

  • Developing wireframes
  • Designing user scenarios
  • Creating interactive layouts

Working with feedback:

  • Conducting online presentation
  • Collecting comments and requests
  • Making adjustments

Design detailing:

  • Working out visual elements
  • Selection of fonts and colors
  • Creating unique graphic solutions

Day 3: Final Refinements and Delivery

The final day is dedicated to the final touches and preparation for project handover:

Final Refinements:

  • Adjusting design details
  • Optimizing the user experience
  • Checking compliance with requirements

Documentation preparation:

  • Compiling a style guide
  • Description of solutions used
  • Formulation of implementation recommendations

Final presentation:

  • Demonstration of the finished project
  • Answering the client's questions
  • Discussion of next steps

Key Milestones and Client Involvement

Ensuring Collaboration Throughout the Process

A successful design project is always the result of close interaction between the team and the client. At Glow Team, we have created an effective communication system that ensures maximum client involvement in the design process.

Each day of the project starts with a short morning briefing where we discuss the work plan and priorities. The client can follow progress throughout the day through online collaboration tools where we share interim results and gather feedback.

We use:

  • Slack for quick communications and file sharing
  • Zoom for daily video conferencing and presentations
  • Figma for real-time design demonstrations
  • Trello to track progress and tasks

At the end of each day, a wrap-up meeting is held where the team presents the results and gathers comments for the next phase of work. This approach allows for quick adjustments and ensures that the final result fully meets the client's expectations.
